# Doofinder

Doofinder adds AI-powered search and discovery to your PlentyONE Shop: smart search, searchandising, recommendations and analytics.

## Getting started and requirements

You need a PlentyONE Shop (PWA) and a plugin set that is active in the shop. A Doofinder account is created during the setup, with a 15-day free trial.

## Setup

1. Install and activate the plugin in your plugin set, then deploy the set.
2. Open the configuration of the plugin and copy the **Feed URL** and the **Token**.
3. In Doofinder, log in (or sign up), add a store, choose **PlentyONE** and paste the Feed URL and the Token.
4. Add the script address that Doofinder shows under **Custom Code**, **External Scripts & Styles** of your PlentyONE Shop.

Doofinder reads the catalog of your shop and creates a search engine for each language. Manage them, and change how your products are indexed, in the Doofinder admin panel.

## Uninstall

1. Remove the script from **Custom Code**, **External Scripts & Styles** of your PlentyONE Shop.
2. Remove the plugin from your plugin set and deploy the set.

Removing the plugin does not delete your Doofinder account or your search engines; manage them in the Doofinder admin panel.
